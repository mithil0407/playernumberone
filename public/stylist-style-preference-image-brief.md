# Stylist Preference Image Brief

Use clean, consistent reference images for the preference sorter. Each image should show the garment or accessory clearly, ideally on-body or against a plain background, with minimal styling noise. Keep crop, lighting, and scale consistent across each category.

## Tops
- relaxed_oversized: relaxed oversized shirt, tee, or blouse
- fitted_tuck_in: fitted top tucked into a waistband
- structured_button_front: crisp button-front shirt or blouse
- draped_wrap: wrap or draped neckline top
- detailed_collar: collar, neckline, sleeve, or shoulder detail
- clean_minimal: simple minimal top with no strong print

## Bottoms
- wide_leg_trouser: wide-leg tailored trouser
- straight_cigarette: straight or cigarette trouser
- tailored_midi_skirt: structured midi skirt
- wrap_skirt: wrap skirt
- straight_jeans: straight-leg fitted jeans
- bootcut: flare or bootcut trouser/jean

## Outerwear
- structured_blazer: structured blazer with shoulder shape
- longline_coat: longline coat or duster
- denim_jacket: denim jacket
- moto_jacket: leather or moto jacket
- knit_cardigan: soft knit cardigan
- linen_blazer: relaxed linen or unstructured blazer

## Accessories
- structured_bag: structured handbag
- soft_tote: soft tote or slouchy bag
- minimal_jewellery: small, minimal jewellery
- statement_jewellery: large or decorative jewellery
- classic_shoe: classic pump, loafer, ballet flat, or simple sandal
- modern_shoe: modern sculptural shoe, sharp boot, or trend-forward sandal
