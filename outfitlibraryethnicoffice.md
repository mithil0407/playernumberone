## What repeats across these 50

- **Tonal kurta + matching trouser** is the dominant formula (about half the set) — the print or the texture carries it, and nothing is added.
- **Sleeve length is almost always three-quarter** — full sleeves appear on the dressier silk pieces, sleeveless on the busiest prints.
- **The dupatta, when present, hangs unpinned down one shoulder** rather than being draped across the chest — it reads office-neat instead of festive.
- **Third-piece layering** is the office upgrade: a western blazer (3, 6), a Nehru waistcoat (19), a long printed shrug (5, 27), or a heavy printed stole (4, 18, 39).
- **High side slits and ankle-length trousers** in almost every look — the proportion that keeps a long kurta from looking heavy.
- **Structured leather totes over ethnic bags** — every single office look uses a western handbag, never a potli.
- **Print on one piece only** — if the kurta is printed, the bottom is plain; if both are printed, they are the same print (a co-ord), never two different ones.
