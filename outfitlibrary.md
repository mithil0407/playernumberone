# ICONIK Outfit Library

The single outfit library for the women's Blueprint. One outfit per numbered line:

`**N.** top / layer / bottom / shoes / accessories. *Styling: how it is worn.*`

- Write `no layer` when there is no jacket or third piece, `dress` in the bottom slot of any one-piece (dress, jumpsuit, saree), and `not visible` when the shoes are unknown.
- Separate accessories with commas. Never use " / " inside a piece; that separates the five slots.
- The grey comment under each outfit records where it came from. Keep it; it is how reviews and reports trace an outfit back.
- Add new outfits at the end of the right section with the next free number. Do not renumber existing outfits.
- Sections are ordered by priority: Stylist picks first, then the Pinterest board, Ethnic, and the looks picked from the women library. Inside each, the ### heading is the capsule (Professional, Social, Everyday or Occasion).
- The app reads only these four ## sections, by exact name. Anything under ## Needs fixing, or under a misspelt heading, is ignored until it is moved into a real section.
