## Patterns across the board

A few formulas repeat often enough to be worth naming:

- **Waistcoat-as-top** (8, 134, 220, 243, 325, 338, 350, 354, 362, 419, 464, 469, 475, 476, 479, 495, 497, 516, 578, 590, 594, 610, 627, 637, 638, 645, 654, 675, 706) — buttoned with nothing underneath, tucked or worn over the waistband.
- **Blazer + tucked-and-belted top + wide-leg bottom** — the single most repeated office formula on the board.
- **Peplum / wrap tops with a side tie** — used constantly to mark a waist without tucking.
- **Print kept to one half** — animal print, polka dot or floral on the bottom with a flat colour on top, or the reverse; almost never both.
- **Shoe or bag matched exactly to the top** — a recurring finishing move (48, 172, 248, 317, 353, 362, 400, 466, 532, 600, 645, 694).
- **Tuck vocabulary** — full tuck, front-only half tuck, front knot, and the shirt knotted at the hem all appear repeatedly and are the main way volume gets controlled.
- **Sleeve vocabulary** — rolled to the elbow on shirts, pushed up on blazers, gathered into a cuff on balloon sleeves.
