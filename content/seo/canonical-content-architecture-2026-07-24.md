The methodology URLs own definition intent: “what is…”, process, scope, limitations and how the method is used. Other pages should summarise the method briefly and link to the relevant canonical page instead of reproducing a second full definition.

## Search intent ownership

- `/methodology`: describes how ICONIK's three frameworks relate.
- `/methodology/*`: owns the definition and limitations of each proprietary framework.
- `/colour-analysis`: owns the broad “colour analysis for Indian skin” topic and routes readers to focused colour guides.
- `/body-type-styling`: owns the broad “body type styling India” topic and routes readers to proportion-specific guides.
- `/style-guides`: owns use-case and occasion navigation.
- `/blog`: contains distinct editorial analysis only. It must not republish methodology definitions.
- `/tools/*` and `/free-colour-analysis-quiz`: provide participatory starting points and must state that outputs are guidance, not diagnoses.

## Internal-link rules

1. Link directly to canonical destinations; do not link through a redirect.
2. Every methodology page links to `/methodology`, its relevant pillar hub and the other two methods.
3. Every pillar hub links to its canonical methodology page near the first definition.
4. Application guides link back to one relevant pillar hub and, when the method is named, its canonical methodology page.
5. Promotional calls to action link to `/offer-2699`, not the homepage.
6. Breadcrumbs reflect the hierarchy above.

## Editorial safeguards

- Describe GSP, CHM and FAA as proprietary styling frameworks, not medical, biomechanical or scientifically validated diagnostic systems.
- Avoid deterministic promises such as “exact”, “guaranteed”, “always flattering” or “systematically fails” unless the statement is supported and appropriately qualified.
- “Plus size” is a size range, not a body-shape category.
- Vein colour and jewellery preference are weak secondary undertone clues; controlled draping in stable light is the stronger at-home method.
- Show an updated date and stylist reviewer on central guides.
- Use ICONIK LLP as the legal entity and Jasmine Rana as Co-Founder and Head Stylist.
