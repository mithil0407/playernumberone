## Article-wide consistency check

- All selected images use one educational idea per canvas.
- Slate and ivory modes alternate through each page.
- Poppins is used for educational hierarchy; the ICONIK wordmark remains a small refined serif.
- Humans are fictional South Asian subjects with natural skin texture and body proportions.
- Comparisons do not slim bodies, lighten skin, or treat a body or face as the problem.
- Service images contain no prices, buttons, purchase prompts, invented locations, or unverified claims.
- Facial visuals explicitly avoid clinical, biometric, symmetry, beauty-score, or attractiveness claims.
- All final assets are WebP at quality 88 and are stored in `public/images/seo`.
