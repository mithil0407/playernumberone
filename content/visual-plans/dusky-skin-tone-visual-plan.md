# 1. CONTENT VISUAL DIAGNOSIS

Primary page: `/colour-analysis/best-colours-dusky-skin`

Primary search intent: understand what “dusky skin tone” means and choose clothing colours by undertone, depth, and contrast.

Reader decisions the visuals must make easier:

- Separate surface depth from undertone.
- Compare warm, cool, neutral, and olive signals without changing a person’s complexion.
- Judge fabric near the face rather than relying on generic colour lists.
- Choose white versus ivory and gold versus silver.
- Save a compact testing method for use with a real wardrobe.

The article is approximately 1,460 words, so the ICONIK visual system calls for 7–11 purposeful visuals. This first production set contains seven: one hero, three comparisons, two palette applications, and one saveable summary.
