# CONTENT VISUAL DIAGNOSIS

Search intent: Understand what “dusky skin tone” means and choose clothing colours by undertone, depth, contrast, whites, and metals.

Target reader: Indian / South Asian women with medium-to-deep brown skin who have received contradictory colour advice.

Core promise: Replace the idea of one “dusky palette” with a controlled, repeatable method for testing colour harmony.

Most visual concepts:

- Surface depth and undertone are different variables.
- A fair drape test changes only the fabric.
- Warm and cool palettes need different neutral anchors.
- White, ivory, gold, and silver should be chosen by facial clarity.
- The four-step Glow Test can be repeated with a real wardrobe.

Recommended image count: Seven visuals for an approximately 1,460-word article.

Visual system: ICONIK v6. Slate `#94A6AD` and ivory `#F7F3ED`; Poppins educational typography; realistic South Asian subjects; small serif ICONIK wordmark; physical swatch cards; rounded frosted-glass teaching card; one lesson per canvas.

Production note: Images 01–03 were generated before the user requested that generated outputs no longer be used as references. Images 04–07 were generated independently. Future regeneration should use the written v6 specification only, with no generated-image reference.
